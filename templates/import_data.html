Expected format

A semicolon-delimited export with a header row. These columns are used when present:

  • cname, cusip, market — company identity
  • disclosure_date, start_date, year — incident timing
  • ransomware, databreach, operational_disruptions, severe — 0/1 flags
  • records_lost, breach_cost — numbers (scientific notation OK)
  • breach_information_type, dataset — categories

cname and disclosure_date are required. industry and country aren't in this format, so they're generated for chart variety.
